# Short Book and Research Companion

## Reading contract

Both editions retain the same 22 chapters and five appendices. Chapter numbers
and appendix letters correspond. Every short chapter points to the expanded
PDF's named destination and gives its evidence status in plain language.

The short edition is a first contemplative draft. Existing expanded chapter
bodies, code, bibliography, and backups are preserved. Many companion entries
remain open research; their short counterparts do not supply missing proofs.

Paths are relative to the repository root. These pairs are editorial sources,
not two independently authoritative protocol specifications. Technical claims
are specified and assessed in the companion and research ledgers.

## Executable routes

Run these commands inside the documented development container's /workspace.
The browser build uses isolated snapshots through container/tasks.sh.

- RPS: make -C demostrations demo_rps
- Tic-Tac-Toe public checker: make -C demostrations demo_ttt
- Tic-Tac-Toe disclosed binding: make -C demostrations demo_ttt_bind
- Poker teaching animation: make -C demostrations demo_poker
- Hash examples: make -C demostrations demo_hash
- Browser: bash container/tasks.sh serve; choose Demos.

For deterministic fixtures and adversarial checks, see tests/ and test-vectors/.
These routes are existing implementations, not evidence for the still-open
private-proof, fairness, collusion, or institutional questions.
