# Execution Plan: Complete Research Draft Assembly

## Status

Complete as of 2026-08-01. This plan repaired the difference between the
repository's full research corpus and the narrow two-chapter PDF previously
described as a full draft.

## Objective

Build one honest, balanced manuscript in which every current content source is
visible without pretending that every source is mature. The book will contain:

1. a front-matter guide explaining evidence-status labels;
2. five parts following the Research Charter's provisional architecture;
3. the mature Trust, Rock--Paper--Scissors, Tic-Tac-Toe, Poker, and Hash
   chapters;
4. the Introduction after bounded claim repair;
5. every short research fragment as a separately titled chapter or appendix;
6. a single bibliography at the end; and
7. no placeholder appendix or hidden chapter source.

The output is a complete research draft, not a claim that every chapter is
complete.

## Scientific and Editorial Boundary

- Chapter presence means preservation and visibility, not validation,
  security, novelty, or completion.
- Original intuitions remain visible and are classified as author's intuition,
  design principle, open problem, research fragment, or placeholder.
- The Introduction's universal quantum, information-theoretic, moral, and
  anti-lying claims must be weakened into hopes or bounded research questions.
- Poker remains a centralized educational toy. The assembly must not promote
  its digests, commutative-encryption sketch, or in-memory transcript into a
  malicious-security, privacy, binding, hiding, or distributed-protocol claim.
- No new theorem, primitive, citation, cryptographic algorithm, dependency, or
  novelty claim is authorized.  The Poker demo's user-facing legacy labels may
  be narrowed to match the chapter's existing single-process toy boundary;
  internal algorithms and APIs remain unchanged.
- No backup file is included or edited.
