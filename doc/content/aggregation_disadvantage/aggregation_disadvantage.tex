\chapter{Aggregation Disadvantage}
\label{chap:aggregation-disadvantage}

\researchstatus{Research intuition}{The aggregation rule and value function
have not yet been formalized.}

Sometimes, when allies join together, their strengths may average, or only the
maximum may remain, rather than their values adding. Separation may benefit
both when the scarce object is custody, territory, attention, or beauty.

Two beautiful things placed together do not necessarily make a third thing
with twice the beauty. The open problem is to identify when cooperation should
preserve separation instead of demanding merger.
