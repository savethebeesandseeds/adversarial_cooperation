\chapter{Defining Victory}
\label{chap:defining-victory}

\researchstatus{Author's intuition and design principle}{This chapter states a
moral orientation for the project. It is not yet a formal game or theorem.}

Victory is not the defeat of the adversaries.

There is a confusion between winning and making others lose:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Making others lose can weaken the whole.
  \item Making others lose can become an attack on the self.
\end{itemize}

The research question is how a game or protocol should represent a victory
that preserves the possibility of future cooperation.
