\chapter{Conflict Arbitrage}
\label{chap:conflict-arbitrage}

\researchstatus{Institutional design intuition}{No formal game, equilibrium,
or cryptographic construction is currently given.}

It may be easier to promote cooperation where there is mutual terrain in which
to expand or grow. There may be value in the space between positions that no
party has yet occupied.

The original note describes arbitration as the exploitation of game
inefficiencies: extracting value while restoring equilibrium. The open task is
to define the parties, the unoccupied value, the permitted intervention, and
who bears its risks.
