\chapter{Byzantine Agreement and Its Limits}
\label{short:byzantine-generals}

When messages conflict, what would it mean for parties to agree? They must
name what may be decided, whose messages are recognized, how long silence
can last, and how many participants may deviate.

Agreement, a valid decision, and eventual completion each ask something
different of a system. The intended chapter will examine their conditions
and limits.

For now this place in the book holds an unwritten question. No construction
has been supplied here. Keeping the gap visible allows the inquiry to
continue without borrowing certainty from a familiar name.
