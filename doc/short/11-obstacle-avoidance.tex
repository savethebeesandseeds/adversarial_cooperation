\chapter{Joint Secure Obstacle Avoidance}
\label{short:obstacle-avoidance}

The note begins with points in three dimensions, then reaches for a further
coordinate: perhaps avoiding an obstacle requires knowing something beyond
where it lies.

The sentence about that extra dimension was never completed. Its intended
meaning remains an author's question. Before coordinating movement, we would
need to describe the geometry, uncertainty, private information, and what a
joint decision reveals.

There may be a cooperation problem here: finding room to move without
requiring everyone to disclose every position or constraint. The companion
preserves that direction. It has not yet defined a private planning protocol
or established that a proposed movement avoids harm.
