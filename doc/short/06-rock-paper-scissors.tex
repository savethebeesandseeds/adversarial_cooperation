\chapter{Two Oracles Play Rock--Paper--Scissors}
\label{short:rock-paper-scissors}

The original question imagines two oracles who can foresee each other's
move. The companion studies a narrower difficulty: whoever learns first may
choose afterward.

The educational protocol asks both parties to commit before either reveals.
Each later opening is checked against the earlier commitment. The intended
protection depends on computational assumptions and an authenticated channel,
set out in the companion.

One difficulty survives. After receiving the first reveal, a player can learn
the outcome and refuse to reveal in return. The demonstration includes this
failure. A fixed choice still leaves a choice about continuing.

\shortlimit{This protocol cannot compel a participant to finish the round.}
