\chapter{Non-Repudiation and Its Limits}
\label{short:non-repudiation}

What should a party be unable to deny, and before whom?

This question asks for more than a name for a cryptographic instrument. We
need to distinguish a signature, authentication, evidence, attribution, and
the institution that interprets a record. Which claim is being made at each
step? What would count as a reason to challenge it?

This appendix is a place reserved for those distinctions. Before asking a
record to settle a disagreement, let us say precisely what it is meant to
establish, and what questions remain for the people reading it.
