\chapter{Fighting Dissipates Strength}
\label{short:fighting-dissipates-strength}

When one fights, one displays strength and gives hints about that strength.
The expenditure includes what another party learns: where a response begins,
what it can do, and perhaps where it ends.

This is a reason to examine the cost of a contest beyond its immediate
outcome. What capability remains afterward? What has the encounter revealed?

Restraint poses its own question. When does it preserve the possibility of
cooperation, and when does silence merely permit harm? The chapter begins
with this tension, without pretending that every confrontation should have
the same answer.
