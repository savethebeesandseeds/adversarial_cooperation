\chapter{The Helmsman's Game}
\label{short:helmsmans-game}

Imagine a diplomatic conversation whose moves help steer a shared future
without breaking the foundations and agreements of those taking part.

What counts as a move? What may remain unspoken? How can the participants
recognize a change of course without discovering that their agreement has
silently become another agreement?

The name offers an image of direction, but the game still needs its rules.
Its purpose must become clear enough to distinguish an agreed destination
from one imposed along the way, and to recognize when the conversation has
failed.

\shortlimit{Cryptography does not decide which destination the participants should accept.}
