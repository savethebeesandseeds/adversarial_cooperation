\chapter{Secure Multi-Party Optimization}
\label{short:multiparty-optimization}

Parties may wish to choose together while keeping their inputs private. The
proposed direction is to optimize a shared objective through joint
computation.

Before asking how well the computation works, ask what it is asked to
improve. Who chose the objective? What does each participant contribute, and
what must the result reveal? Would an improvement by this measure benefit
the parties who must live with it?

The companion currently holds only the research topic. Its function, private
inputs, verification rule, and adversary remain undefined. A precise
optimization problem is still needed before there is a protocol to examine.

\shortlimit{Private computation alone cannot establish that the chosen objective is just.}
\companionref{12}{chapter.12}{An open topic; no optimization protocol or implementation is supplied.}
