\chapter{ISO/IEC Standards Queue}
\label{short:standards}

Precision asks us to name the document on which a claim depends. A family
name alone leaves the reader searching for its edition, its part, and the
passage that matters.

The book keeps a queue of standards to investigate: encryption algorithms,
authenticated encryption, lightweight cryptography, and hash functions.
These entries mark work to do. Their relevant editions and claims still
need verification.

Let a reference lead somewhere definite. Until we have followed it, the
name belongs among the questions we carry forward, rather than among the
answers on which a construction relies.

\shortlimit{Naming a standard does not establish that a construction meets its requirements.}
