\chapter{Interactive Secure Stag Hunt}
\label{short:stag-hunt}

The original note places two impulses beside one another: sometimes we want
to hurt; sometimes we want to grow. It asks whether algorithmic cooperation
can help distinguish when to choose one action and when to choose the other.

The symbols in that note have no settled game beneath them. We still need
to name the parties, the available actions, the consequences, and what each
party knows. The title records an ambition whose meaning must be worked out.

Keep the question open long enough to understand it. A mechanism should not
quietly supply the judgment the question has left unresolved.

\shortlimit{Cryptography cannot decide when an action that harms is justified.}
