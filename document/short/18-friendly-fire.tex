\chapter{Friendly Fire}
\label{short:friendly-fire}

How can one restrain an ally without turning cooperation into domination?

A friendly tackle may be mistaken for defiance. It may also be badly
executed. The ally may wish to return to the very position from which it was
moved. Calling the intervention friendly does not settle any of these
questions.

Yet there is also an unseen cost of never intervening. What harm proceeds
because an ally remains silent?

Keep both questions alive: how correction can be offered, and how correction
can itself be challenged. A shared purpose does not spare us the difficulty
of distinguishing help, mistake, and coercion.
