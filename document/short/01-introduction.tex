\chapter{Introduction}
\label{short:introduction}

Assume that an adversary may be smarter than us. Hope that understanding may
bring kindness, but build no promise upon that hope alone.

Parties may share a purpose while protecting different interests. Cooperation
asks what they can undertake together without surrendering every secret or
placing every decision in another's hands. Begin with a small question: what
must be known, what can remain private, and what happens when someone departs?

These pages invite correction. A missing assumption or a successful attack
can teach us where a promise ends. Let us investigate how to make war
optional. Peace is the purpose of this inquiry; it remains a human task.
