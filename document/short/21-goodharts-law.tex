\chapter{Addressing Goodhart's Law}
\label{short:addressing-goodharts-law}

Suppose we make harmony the target. Have we left room for the disagreement
through which the shared purpose might change?

An objective deserves scrutiny even when it is carefully measured. Perhaps
its flaws should be expected, their discovery welcomed, and its revision
made possible. Perhaps the system should reward those who show where its
measure has ceased to serve its purpose.

Hard problems require humble solutions. Leave room for appeal, succession,
and expiration. An inflexible system that cannot end may preserve its target
long after losing the reason for that target.

We do not yet know how to solve this problem.
