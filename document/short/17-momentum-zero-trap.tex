\chapter{Momentum-Zero Trap}
\label{short:momentum-zero-trap}

Those who are not allowed to participate cannot show what their participation
might bring. The game may lose a sophistication it has never learned to see.

One may duplicate zero many times and still have zero. This is the image of
the trap: a beginning that demands the momentum only a beginning could give.

How do gears break momentum zero? What invitation, initial endowment, or
change of rules lets a participant begin?

Before praising the fairness of continued competition, ask how entry becomes
possible, and whether the starting conditions have been allowed to become a
permanent sentence.
