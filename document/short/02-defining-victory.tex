\chapter{Defining Victory}
\label{short:defining-victory}

Victory is not the defeat of the adversaries.

There is a confusion between winning and making others lose. Making others
lose can weaken the whole; it can become an attack on the self. Before
celebrating an outcome, consider what has been diminished along with the
opponent.

What remains possible afterward? Can the parties still undertake something
worth doing together? Can they disagree without making every shared purpose
impossible?

This is a moral orientation for the book. The open research question is how
to give it a place in a game or protocol: a meaning of victory that preserves
the possibility of future cooperation.
