\chapter{Poker Without Revealing the Cards}
\label{short:poker}

The table needs a common game while each player keeps a private hand. The
dealer's work therefore contains several promises: a complete deck, a shared
deal, private cards, and a later account of what was held.

The original question reaches further: can a player establish a win without
showing the hand? It also encounters a tension. Withholding cards even from
their owners might frustrate association, but would leave ordinary betting
without the information it needs.

The present demonstration makes dealing stages inspectable inside one
process. It exposes every player's state there. Private winner verification,
collusion resistance, and a complete distributed game remain open.
