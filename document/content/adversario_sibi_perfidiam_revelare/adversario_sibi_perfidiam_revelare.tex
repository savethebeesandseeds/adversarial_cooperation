\chapter{Telling an Adversary It Is Wrong}
\label{chap:telling-adversary-wrong}

\researchstatus{Open problem}{The Latin working title is retained in the source
path pending linguistic review. No protocol, leakage function, or adversary
model has yet been selected.}

Can one tell an adversary that it is wrong using secret information, without
requiring the adversary to reveal its own secret information?

The first task is to define who holds the secret, who learns the warning, what
predicate is evaluated, and what the warning itself reveals.
