The original note places parties or obstacles as points in vector coordinates.
One may imagine a point in three-dimensional space, written \(q_{j,3}\), and
then ask whether an additional coordinate \(q_{j,3+1}\) should represent
something that must also be avoided.

The note began to describe that extra dimension as a coefficient of what has
been explored, but the sentence was never completed. The missing meaning is
preserved as an open authorial question rather than silently invented here.
