\chapter{Secure Multi-Party Optimization}
\label{chap:secure-multiparty-optimization}

\researchstatus{Open research topic}{The objective, private inputs, leakage,
verification rule, and adversary model are not yet defined.}

The proposed direction is functional optimization using secure multi-party
computation. Secure computation of an objective would not by itself establish
that the parties chose a just or mutually beneficial objective.
