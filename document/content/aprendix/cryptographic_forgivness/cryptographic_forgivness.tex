\chapter{Cryptographic Forgiveness}
\label{app:cryptographic-forgiveness}

\researchstatus{Open problem}{The project cannot presently prove that an
adversarial machine deleted every copy of a file or database record.}

The original aspiration was a protocol through which two or more parties show
that they have deleted a file or record. A narrower defensible direction is to
study destruction of encryption keys under explicit storage, backup, hardware,
collusion, and attestation assumptions. Copies, caches, screenshots, and
compromised endpoints remain residual risks.
