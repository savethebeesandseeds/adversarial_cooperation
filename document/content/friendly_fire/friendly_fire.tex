\chapter{Friendly Fire}
\label{chap:friendly-fire}

\researchstatus{Question outline}{No formal model currently distinguishes
correction, coercion, defiance, mistake, and justified refusal.}

How can one challenge or restrain an ally without converting cooperation into
domination? The original note asks:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item What happens if the ally wants to return to the position from which a
  friendly tackle moved it?
  \item What happens when an ally confuses a friendly tackle with defiance?
  \item What happens when the tackle is badly executed?
  \item What is the unseen cost of never tackling?
\end{enumerate}
