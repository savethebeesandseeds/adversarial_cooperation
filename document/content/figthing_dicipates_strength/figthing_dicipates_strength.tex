\chapter{Fighting Dissipates Strength}
\label{chap:fighting-dissipates-strength}

\researchstatus{Design intuition}{No leakage model or strategic game has yet
been defined.}

When one fights, one displays strength and also gives hints about that
strength. In an adversarial setting, the revelation itself may be costly. The
open question is when restraint preserves capability and when silence merely
permits harm.
