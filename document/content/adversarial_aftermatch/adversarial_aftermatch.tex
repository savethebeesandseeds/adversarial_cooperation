\chapter{Adversarial Aftermath}
\label{chap:adversarial-aftermath}

\researchstatus{Governance notes}{No settlement protocol or stability result
is currently established.}

Unity can degrade in the absence of a common adversary. This chapter asks what
happens after the contest that organized cooperation has ended.

The original notes preserve two warnings:

\begin{itemize}
  \item Never make victory perpetual.
  \item The instrument of defeat may need to be invoked by the winners;
  otherwise victory may remain unstable and meet resistance.
\end{itemize}

The open work is to translate these intuitions into termination, sunset,
appeal, succession, restoration, and negotiated-exit mechanisms without
granting permanent exceptional power.
