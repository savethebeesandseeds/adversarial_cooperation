\chapter{Momentum-Zero Trap}
\label{chap:momentum-zero-trap}

\researchstatus{Institutional research note}{The relation to poverty traps is
suggestive but has not been modeled or cited in this chapter.}

A momentum-zero trap is suffered by those who are not allowed to participate.
One may miss the sophistication that excluded participants would add to the
game.

In a competitive game, starting from zero can be a severe disadvantage. One
may duplicate zero many times and still have zero: zero momentum can appear
unbreakable.

The author has long wondered how gears break momentum zero. The chapter's open
question is which initial endowment, credit, invitation, or protocol transition
allows a participant to begin without freezing an unjust initial state.
